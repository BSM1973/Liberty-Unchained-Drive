# v0.4 Extreme DSP Precision

RMS compensation uses exponentially averaged reference and wet energy with a 300 ms time constant and a 180 ms correction time constant. Gain correction is bounded to -18/+12 dB and disabled on near silence. It operates per processed channel, not perceptual LUFS. CI adds a steady-state RMS convergence and silence check at four rates. Main engine changes crossfade two preallocated DSP paths for 25 ms; main controls and per-band gains/mix are smoothed. Oversampling retains JUCE integer-latency high-quality IIR filters, with offline override and a fade-in after changes, not seamless oversampling switching. CPU telemetry measures callback time divided by audio buffer duration, not system CPU. Analyzer shows actual mono-summed input/output signals; waveform is not delay aligned and antiphase stereo may cancel. Presets retain names/count but use circuit-specific values; no subjective listening certification. Linear-phase crossover, quantified aliasing benchmarks, host latency measurement and all topology/automation transitions remain future validation work. No claims of commercial readiness.
